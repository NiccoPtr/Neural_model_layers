### Interaction attentive/action components



##### Attention bias



* The cortical layer dedicated to the attentive component will exert a moderate/strong influence onto the cortical motor components, thus leading to perform the action toward the object with the attentive bias

&#x09;

&#x09;- This bias could to either always or often modulate motor cortex activity, establishing independence of cortical motor component activity from attentive component



##### Action bias



* The cortical layer dedicated to the motor component will exert a moderate/strong influence onto the cortical attentive components, thus leading the attention toward the object which has been selected by the action to perform



&#x09;- This bias could to either always or often modulate motor cortex activity, establishing independence of cortical attentive component activity from attentive component



##### Bidirectional bias



* Both cortical components will have equal capacity to modulate the other's activity





### How to deal with incongruent scenarios



Motor and attentive components of the cortex will be independent but still will interact between each other modulating their activities. The fact that they will be structurally connected but independent could lead to scenarios where the action selected might not be the one that can be performed at the object toward which the attention is focusing.

This scenario would be handled in one these manners:



1. In case of incongruency, the action will be considered as not valid, thus no reward will be released

2. In case of incongruency, the motor cortex activity should be capable to redirect the attention toward the object at which the action can be performed

- This would require the motor cortex to exert a strong modulation toward the attentive component of the cortex

3. In case of incongruency, the attentive component cortex activity should be capable to modulate the motor cortex activity such that the proper action will be selected



&#x20;  - This would require the attentive component of the cortex to exert a strong modulation toward the motor cortex
