# Instruction for PIT Model



#### Scheduling:



* Phase 1 --> Pavlovian Conditioning



&#x09;	- Teach model to learn association between Light\_1/2 and Reward\_1/2

&#x09;	- The PFCd\_PPC won't guide the attention since the Light\_1/2 will be passively presented within the environment through the scheduling



* Phase 2 --> Instrumental Learning



&#x09;	- Classic training phase with Manipulanda\_1/2 and Reward\_1/2

&#x09;	- Attention guided by PFCd\_PPC



* Phase 3 --> PIT Test



&#x09;	- Extinction phase, Reward\_1/2 won't be delivered in case of successful actions

&#x09;	- Attention will guide action toward the Manipulanda\_1/2

&#x09;	- 1/3 of the trial will be without Light\_1/2 presentation

&#x09;	- The remaining 2/3 will be divided into 50% presentation of Light\_1/2 within the first 100 steps of the trial



* Phase 3\_B --> PIT Test



&#x09;	- Extinction phase, Reward\_1/2 won't be delivered in case of successful actions

&#x09;	- Attention will guide action toward the Manipulanda\_1/2

&#x09;	- Each 1/3 of the trials will have a condition:

&#x09;		

&#x09;		- One manipulanda (Lever) and no Lights

&#x09;		- One manipulanda (Lever) and congruent Light

&#x09;		- One manipulanda (Lever) and incongruent Light



&#x09;			-Swap from Lever to Chain between subjects, not within



#### Expected results:



* We expect a 50-50 choice of either Manipulanda\_1/2 during the first 1/3 of the PIT Test, meanwhile a bias toward the Manipulanda associated with the same Reward of the presented Light during the remaining 2/3 of the PIT Test

# Specific PIT brain areas



Main areas:



* Basolateral amygdala (BLA) → encodes/uses the specific predicted outcome.
* Nucleus accumbens shell (NAc shell) → translates that outcome-specific information into increased instrumental responding.
* VTA dopamine system also contributes.
* Dorsomedial striatum (DMS) has also been implicated in specific PIT.
